## 📖 Overview

Symptom Navigator is designed to provide users with quick and accessible preliminary healthcare guidance. By entering symptoms through an intuitive interface, users can receive AI-powered insights, possible health condition suggestions, and recommendations for the next steps.

The platform further enhances accessibility by helping users locate nearby healthcare facilities including hospitals, clinics, pharmacies, and emergency centers.

> **Note:** This application is intended for informational purposes only and does not replace professional medical advice.

## 🔍 How It Works

### Step 1: Enter Symptoms
Users provide symptoms through an interactive interface.

### Step 2: AI Analysis
The system evaluates symptoms and identifies possible health-related conditions.

### Step 3: Generate Insights
Relevant recommendations and precautionary advice are presented.

### Step 4: Find Nearby Care
Users can access nearby:
- Hospitals
- Clinics
- Pharmacies
- Emergency Centers

### Step 5: Take Action
Based on the analysis and recommendations, users can decide whether professional medical consultation is necessary.
